# ADR 0040 — The Unified Energy Market (e/t), the Economy Decision Seam, and the Offline Economy Simulator

- **Status:** Decided
- **Date:** 2026-07-01
- **Deciders:** operator
- **Related:** ADR 0033 (sim-core substrate + §D5.4 e/t objective + the numeric w-bid movement lane this mirrors), ADR 0038 (`room_net_roi` e/t accounting), ADR 0032 (`value_e` currency + P-AUCTION assignment precedent), ADR 0006 (server-backed eval harness / colony-health — the live complement), ADR 0007 (hauling pillar), ADR 0011 (spawn orchestration pillar), ADR 0016 (HUD — bid observability), ADR 0015 (gate policy); `todo.md:18`; `missions/haul.rs:299` ("TODO: Make sure there is handling for starvation/bootstrap mode.")
- **One line:** Under collapse, WORK creeps drive-by-repair decayed roads with the very energy ticketed for spawn/extension refill, and the delivery path doesn't even honor its own priority tiers — because the bot has no common notion of what energy is *worth* where. The end-state: every energy sink and every spawn request bids in the one ratified currency (energy-equivalent per tick), tickets carry numeric bids, a per-room CPU-bounded assignment optimizes bid-weighted service, and the whole decision layer is extracted behind a pure DTO seam (the combat-decision pattern) so the live bot and a new offline economy simulator (sim spawn / economy / source+mineral / controller / lab systems over `screeps-sim-core`) run the *same* code — with bootstrap recovery time and time-to-RCL(N) as the headline metrics that calibrate the market's constants before it goes default-on. A small flag-gated repair stress gate is the interim stopgap.

**Division of ownership.** This ADR owns: the energy *pricing* layer (all sink and spawn-request bids in e/t), the transfer *assignment* policy (matching carriers to tickets — the priority/assignment slice of ADR 0007's territory, superseding its implicit nearest-wins behavior), the *currency conversion* of spawn-request priorities (f32 bands → e/t bids; ADR 0011 keeps queue mechanics, group spawning, and orchestration), the economy decision seam (`EconomyView`/`EconomyIntent` + `screeps-econ-decision`), and the offline economy simulator crates + metrics. It does **not** own: movement (ADR 0033 — reused via `rover_driver`; distance estimates use existing pathing services, no one-off algorithms), the server-backed harness + `colony_health` (ADR 0006 — the live validation instrument), combat tactics/sizing (ADR 0019/0020/0022 — military spawn bids are *priced through* the already-ratified §D5.4 `w`/`value_e` machinery, not redesigned here), terminal/market credit strategy (ADR 0012 — `fairvalue.rs` untouched; mineral-sale-as-recovery enters only as a sim lever). Per EP-6.14 no new physics substrate is minted: 0033 explicitly reserved `screeps-sim-core` "for future non-combat sims (economy, hauling, lifecycle)" — this ADR is that consumer.

## Context

### §0 The pain, and why now

Operator report (2026-07-01): *"Under room collapse or stress situations, we're not refilling extensions and spawns fast enough. Instead we're opportunistically healing roads which is low expected value. We need to save the energy for extension refills to rebootstrap the room."* Plus the measurement ask: a simulator + metric for *bootstrap recovery time* and *initial room build / RCL rush speed*, with *"a sim spawn system, sim economy system, sim lab system, sim source and mineral system."* And the design directives (operator, same day): **"WFV changes are trivial and not a concern. Target the desired end-state, not compromised design. If needed we can change or refactor any system (for example to make the economy decisions pure for testing similar to other systems)."**

Refill is the compounding investment — energy in extensions buys bigger creeps, which buy income — while road repair under collapse is near-worthless. Today the bot leaks bootstrap energy into roads through five independent code paths, the hauler delivery path ignores the priority refill already registers at, and *nothing anywhere* expresses that a post-wipe extension refill is worth an order of magnitude more than a road top-up. The acknowledged gap is a literal TODO (`missions/haul.rs:299`). The deeper diagnosis: the economy has **no common value currency** — priorities are hand-ranked enums and f32 bands, scattered ad-hoc thresholds approximate value, and each new sink re-litigates its rank. Combat already solved this class of problem twice (ADR 0032 `value_e` EV auction; ADR 0033 §D5.4 numeric w-bids on the movement lane); the civilian economy is the last major system without it.

### §2 The constraint litany

- Single-threaded WASM; per-tick CPU budget **including intents**. The matching optimization (§D3) is new work on the hottest per-tick path — it gets an explicit measured CPU gate, and near-optimal beats optimal-but-slow by decree (operator, interview 2026-07-01).
- **Serialization is NOT a design constraint** (operator directive 2026-07-01: "WFV changes are trivial"): reset-anytime per EP-5.1, no migration paths, loudness is the requirement. The ticket reshape therefore costs a `WORLD_FORMAT_VERSION` bump and a loud full reset, and that price does not shape the design.
- **Per-tick optimal over hysteresis** (standing directive): the market is the purest expression — no modes, no latching; stress behavior *emerges* from prices recomputed each tick. The flap-rate metric is the observation channel that would ever justify damping.
- Determinism (EP-6.13): bids are **quantized integers** before any comparison or ordering; no HashMap iteration reaches a decision; the sim fence is spread-0 from the first commit.
- Purity (EP-6.2/6.1): decision logic below the seam takes DTOs only; kernels ship kernel/fixture tests in the same commit.
- Pathfinding stays in the pathing system — the market supplies *pricing*; distance estimates come from existing services (linear range today, `PathfinderService` where already used).
- EP-4.6 measured-not-asserted constants (the sim tournament is the calibration instrument); EP-2.2 parity-first plumbing; EP-2.6 one implementation per concern (bands, tiers, ad-hoc thresholds, and the S1 stopgap are all *deleted* at adoption); EP-6.7 paired-diff gates.
- Engine ground truth is `docs/references/engine-mechanics.md` — every sim mechanic cites it.

### §3 Scope

**IN:** the e/t sink market (pricing every civilian energy sink + withdraw admission); numeric-bid tickets; per-room bid-optimizing assignment; spawn-request currency conversion (civilian **and** military bids, the latter priced through existing §D5.4 machinery); the `EconomyView`/`EconomyIntent` full decision seam + `screeps-econ-decision`; the S1 interim stopgap; the offline economy sim (crates, four sim systems incl. **full** labs/minerals with boost effects, scenario corpus, metrics); live adoption gates.

**OUT (named owners):** movement/pathing (0033); spawn-queue mechanics + group spawning (0011); combat sizing/tactics and the military `w`/`value_e` *definitions* (0020/0022/0033 §D5.4 — consumed, not changed); terminal credit strategy (0012); server harness (0006); hostile-pressure collapse scenarios.

## Decision

**Adopt the unified e/t energy market as the end-state: every energy sink and every spawn request bids energy-equivalent-per-tick in one currency; tickets carry quantized numeric bids; a per-room, CPU-bounded, near-optimal assignment allocates carrier service to bids; direct withdrawal is admitted against the room's opportunity floor; and the entire economy decision layer moves behind a pure DTO seam consumed identically by the live bot and the new offline economy simulator — which reproduces the collapse disease, calibrates the market's constants, and gates default-on. The S1 repair stress gate + leak telemetry are the labeled interim.**

**Scope reconciliation with ADRs 0007 and 0011 (operator-ratified).** **(R1)** ADR 0007's Q5 `TransferSnapshot` two-phase hauler matcher is **unified into this ADR**: the immutable per-tick snapshot + pure `(snapshot, …) → tickets` seam lands once here; today's greedy selection is the baseline behind it (0007's replay-diff parity gate is this ADR's seam A/A gate); the market's per-room bid assignment is the candidate that replaces it; 0007 item 4 (route-distance sizing via ADR 0038's machinery) feeds K4 bodies + matching `service_ticks`. **(R2)** ADR 0011's economic orchestrator is **re-scoped**: D2 throughput/energy budget + D7 starvation cure are delivered by this ADR (the market IS the budget; K4 is the cure); 0011 keeps D5 cross-room assist + G3 incubation (consuming this ADR's currency) + its Step-0 spawn-executor quick-wins (landed with this workstream's spawn work to avoid file contention; the spawn comparator is verified-correct — adapt, never reverse). ADR 0041's `mineral_value_e` trust-gated kernel is the shared valuation the lab/boost bids consume — one kernel, coordinated, not duplicated.

*Decision provenance.* Designed via grounded multi-agent synthesis 2026-07-01 (six codebase recon readers; three independent design formulations — per-tick tiers+gates, ledger waterfall, e/t market — plus a sim architecture; adversarial judge), artifacts in `docs/design/assets/0040-synthesis/`. The judge originally ranked tiers+gates first *for v1* on one dominance argument: the market's resolution died at the then-frozen serialized 4-tier `TransferPriority` boundary. The operator lifted that constraint the same day ("WFV trivial; target the desired end-state") and resolved the eight open forks by interview: **(1)** end-state = e/t sink market; **(2)** purification = full decision seam (combat-decision pattern); **(3)** the S1 stopgap ships as the interim; **(4)** tickets get a numeric bid lane; **(5)** spawn currency = **full unification including military** (via the ratified §D5.4 `w`/`value_e` arm); **(6)** matching = keep lazy demand materialization, then **full bid optimization**, "runnable in reasonable CPU constraints — if not exactly optimal, faster near-optimal solutions may be fine"; **(7)** road upkeep = emergent from pricing (trust the currency; traffic-aware imminence required); **(8)** sim labs = **full scope** (boost effects on the economy loop, mineral-sale recovery lever, own metrics).

### §D1 The market: one currency, every sink

All bids are in **energy-equivalent per tick**, the codebase's one ratified cross-goal currency (ADR 0032 `value_e`, 0038 `room_net_roi`, 0033 §D5.4), quantized to integer milli-e/t (`BID_SCALE = 1000`) at the source — floats never reach a comparison. **Storage is the numeraire: depositing to storage bids exactly 1.000.** A sink's bid answers: *marginal energy-equivalent value returned per unit of energy spent here, now* — so under stress the right behavior emerges with zero modes, and every future sink is priced by adding one function, not one subsystem.

Bid functions (v0 shapes — every constant named and sim-swept per EP-4.6; `sink_economics.rs` in `screeps-econ-decision`):

- **Spawn/extension refill** *inherits the bid of what the energy enables*: refill deposits bid `max(1.0, ROI(top energy-blocked spawn request))` where `ROI(request) ≈ w(request) · CREEP_LIFE_TIME / body_cost` — the marginal e/t return per energy invested in that body. Post-wipe, the first harvester's ROI is enormous (it unlocks all income), so refill outbids everything — *which is the rebootstrap priority, derived rather than decreed*. With a full spawn queue satisfied and extensions topped, refill demand registers zero amount and the bid is moot (structural degradation to today). The realized floor is **derived from the banking model** (the instant-spawnability premium), not a swept constant: the literal `max(1000, ROI)` shape above was disconfirmed by the tournament.
- **Repair**: `bid = (rebuild_cost_avoided / repair_cost_remaining) · imminence(hits, wear_rate)` where `wear_rate` = decay **plus observed traffic wear** (trailing hits-delta per structure; decay-only pricing kills trafficked corridors — judge-verified defect, fixed by construction). Rebuild costs ~6× full-repair for a plain road, so imminent death prices high and a 50%-health road prices ~0.3–0.4: roads deliberately ride low when energy has better uses (operator-accepted end-state). Containers add their functional value (harvest throughput carried). Spawns/towers under threat route to the survival override, not the market.
- **Build**: per-energy value of the structure class completing — extensions post-wipe are the compounding investment (capacity → bigger bodies → income) and price high; roads price their movement-savings (the rover corpus already measures e/t per fatigue tile); containers price harvest efficiency. v0 = a per-class table, sim-swept.
- **Upgrade**: `V_UPGRADE` (declared policy constant: the e/t worth of controller progress, with an RCL-unlock step premium near level-up), plus a **hard downgrade override**: clock below threshold escalates outside the market (a survival veto in the §D5.4 gate idiom, not a bid — catastrophe guards don't bid, they veto).
- **Towers**: hostiles present → survival lane (never-shed, effectively top-of-market, EP-4.3); peaceful → maintenance value (low; loses to any real demand — deleting the S4 leak).
- **Labs/terminal/nuker/powerspawn**: priced when reached; lab loading bids the compound pipeline's value via the shared `mineral_value_e` cost-of-production kernel, terminal energy for sends prices via ADR 0012's `fairvalue` when that ADR consumes the lane.
- **Military energy draws** (squad provisioning, boost loading): priced through the existing military `w`/`value_e` machinery — no new definitions here.
- **Withdraw admission (subsumes the ledger design's one unique capability):** every withdraw/pickup ticket carries its *destination sink's* bid. Direct-Use consumers (builders/upgraders pulling from containers/storage) are admitted iff their sink bid ≥ the room's **opportunity floor** = the highest materially-unmet deposit bid. Post-wipe: refill's floor is ~10×, so upgraders stop draining the container the refill hauler needs — withdraw-side competition is priced natively, no waterfall machinery.

**Guardrails (explicit, few):** the downgrade override; the hostile-tower survival lane; spawn-structure repair below 50% (survival). Everything else is a price.

### §D2 Spawn queue joins the currency (full unification)

K4 emits spawn requests as `(role, body, bid_e_t)` — civilian bids from the same ROI math (harvester = income unlocked; hauler = logistics throughput unblocked; builder/upgrader = their sink's value stream), **military bids from the ratified §D5.4 `w(creep)`/`value_e` arm** — one room, one currency for what energy *flows to* and what it *becomes*. The queue mechanism is retained: descending order (now by bid) with **head-of-line energy banking** unchanged — the top *bid* banks income, which is exactly the "save energy for the refill/rebootstrap chain" behavior, derived. `COMBAT_FORMING`'s intent (forming squads must bank ahead of civilian churn) is preserved by construction: a forming squad's `w` is high while the formation window is open, expressed as the `SPAWN_BID_COMBAT_FORMING` anchor. The f32 band constants (`SPAWN_PRIORITY_*`) are **deleted** (EP-2.6); the deliberately-reversed comparator + its pinned test adapt to the bid key (a total-order `u32`). K4 also fixes S6 by design: bodies are sized against actual affordable-now state and priced accordingly, so a 1700-cost replacement miner no longer silently banks a trickle for hundreds of ticks when a 300-cost body has 5× the ROI.

Blast-radius note: this touches combat spawning. The conversion is its own milestone with a combat-eval parity check (the lifecycle harness re-run: forming/refresh behavior unchanged within CI), and `can_afford_military`'s 20% stored reserve is retained as a transition guardrail (decision #2).

### §D3 Matching: lazy materialization, then bid optimization

Demand generation keeps today's lazy per-room materialization (`LazyTransferQueueRooms` — generators flush per transfer-type on first read). What changes is selection: instead of per-creep nearest-wins, a **per-room assignment pass** allocates carriers to tickets to maximize total bid-weighted service rate: edge value `v(carrier, ticket) = quantize(bid · amount / service_ticks)` with `service_ticks` from existing distance services. Operator directive: full bid optimization worth it, **CPU-bounded, near-optimal acceptable**. Concretely:

- **v1 algorithm:** deterministic greedy on globally sorted edge value-density with booking (the existing pending-ticket bookkeeping is the booking layer) — `O(E log E)`, a ½-approximation for weighted matching, byte-deterministic (quantized keys, position tie-breaks).
- **Reference oracle (sim only):** exact assignment (Hungarian / min-cost-flow) to measure the **`match_optimality_gap`** metric — never shipped live.
- **Escalation path:** if the greedy gap is material on realistic corpora, bounded auction-algorithm iterations (the ADR 0032 P-AUCTION idiom) are the next rung; chosen by the measured CPU/quality frontier, not taste (EP-7.2). On the contended corpus the measured pooled gap is **18‰**, well under the ~50‰ escalation threshold — so greedy stands (decision #4).
- **CPU gate:** matching cost per tick measured in-sim (ops proxy) and live (profiling feature) on the 13-room corpus; the budget is **≤ ~50 ops/tick/room** (contended p95 measured at 18), with the hard budget confirmed against live profiling. Multi-room carriers (remote haulers) match in their delivery room's pass.

Mid-flight re-evaluation stays per-tick and cheap: an in-flight carrier keeps its booking unless a strictly-better (quantized, hysteresis-free) assignment exists for its remaining capacity — per-tick optimal, ties break stable.

### §D4 Tickets: the numeric bid lane

`TransferPriority {High, Medium, Low, None}` is **replaced** as the ordering language by a quantized `bid: u32` (milli-e/t) on requests, queue keys, and in-flight tickets — mirroring the rover's numeric `priority_value` w-bid movement lane, so the bot's two request systems share one priority architecture. The serialized ticket shape changes (`TransferWithdrawTicket`/`TransferDepositTicket`/`HaulState`), which costs a `WORLD_FORMAT_VERSION` bump and a loud full reset per EP-5.1 — trivial per the operator directive. The tier enum survives only as a coarse display/label mapping for logs and HUD, and as the tier-interleave fallback behind the bid-native selection path; its home is `screeps-econ-decision::priority` (one implementation). The market needs an observability answer for "why did the hauler skip the lab": one console line per room (top-3 unmet bids + opportunity floor) and a Glance-HUD row (ADR 0016) are part of the live market, not an add-on — an emergent system without a readout is an operations regression (decision #5).

### §D5 The economy decision seam (full purification)

The combat-decision pattern, replicated: a pure, JS-free **`screeps-econ-decision`** crate owns *all* economy decisions — transfer demand + bids (K1), matching/task selection (K2), repair admission (K3), spawn request sets incl. bodies + bids (K4), and build/upgrade target choice — behind `EconomyView` (DTOs: room economy, stores, structures, demand set, creeps) and `EconomyIntent` (the write seam). Missions/jobs become thin adapters: build DTOs from live handles, execute intents (`squad_combat.rs` is the worked example; `screeps-combat-agent::SimView` is the sim-side example). The seam grows consumer-by-consumer (EP-2.3) with A/A parity at each step (EP-6.4), and at end-state there is **exactly one implementation** of economy policy, consumed by the live bot and the sim — mirror drift dissolves, and "make the economy decisions pure for testing similar to other systems" is the operator's ask realized. `EconomySnapshot` plumbing extends to jobs (`Read<>` in `JobSystemData`, the `repair_queue` pattern) as the DTO source. The `spawn_throughput` mirror in `screeps-combat-decision` is superseded by the shared K4/queue kernel; its shared home is `screeps-econ-engine::spawn_queue`, so the econ sim never depends on a combat crate (decision #3).

### §D6 S1 — the interim stopgap (labeled, deleted when the market goes live)

One small flag-gated slice against *current* code, because the leak costs real recovery time while the end-state lands: pure kernel `energy_stress.rs` (`repair_allowance`: `Unrestricted` iff stored ≥ 10k or refill deficit ≤ 10%, else effective repair minimum = `Critical`), applied inside `tick_opportunistic_repair` + the two shared repair selectors (covers every S1 role), plus `get_repairer_priority` and peaceful tower repair; posture keyed to the creep's home/delivery room; kill-switch `features.energy.repair_stress_gate`; paired with the live `repair_leak_e` telemetry counter (energy spent on repair while refill deficit exists, by structure class, via `metrics.rs`). The counter is permanent (it anchors the sim's repro gate and the live validation); the gate is interim scaffolding per EP-2.10, removed when the market's opportunity-floor admission replaces it (EP-10.5). Interim constants are declared interim — the market replaces them, not calibrates them.

Fidelity details that the kernel must respect: telemetry counts ALIVE work parts (engine `repair.js` parity) while deposit accounting keeps the historical total-count arithmetic (so the flag-off path is byte-parity); a multi-home hauler's posture is the delivery room the creep stands in, else the first; and the telemetry condition (ANY deficit) is deliberately *not* the gate condition (10% / 10k), pinned by a divergence test. The S1 allowance kernel outlives the live gate: the sim keeps an S1 arm to score the stopgap against the market.

### §D7 Part B — the offline economy simulator

**Crates** (host-only, zero live CPU), mirroring the 0033 shape: **`screeps-econ-engine`** — the economy overlay on `screeps-sim-core` exactly as `screeps-combat-engine` overlays movement (`EconWorld` embedding `MovementState`; `EconIntents` embedding `MoveIntents` + Harvest/Transfer/Withdraw/Pickup/Build/Repair/UpgradeController/SpawnCreep/RunReaction/Boost; `resolve_econ_tick` calling `resolve_movement` via the `Simulation` trait — "No change to this crate is needed to add a layer"). Intent simultaneity follows the bot's own Pipeline A/D model; deviations from the engine's per-intent pipelines documented. Every constant cites `engine-mechanics.md`/engine source, pinned by a unit test. **`screeps-econ-decision`** — §D5 (the *same crate the live bot ships*). **`screeps-econ-eval`** — scenarios, oracles, metrics, `econ_bench` CLI (`fast|full`, env overrides, per-family + pooled headline, non-zero exit on gate failure), tuning sweep, determinism fence.

**The four sim systems (operator ask, concrete):**
1. **Source & mineral:** pools 3000/1500/4000; 2 e/WORK/t; 300-tick regen **with the first-harvest timer subtlety**; minerals: 4 density tiers {15k,35k,70k,100k}, extractor + 5-tick cooldown, `HARVEST_MINERAL_POWER` 1 mineral/WORK/t (boosted ×3/×5/×7), 50k regen (`MINERAL_REGEN_TIME`), **density re-roll on regen** (seeded from a per-mineral `reroll_seed` counter — no threaded RNG, so the fence stays spread-0).
2. **Spawn:** atomic energy debit (no per-tick drain), spawns-then-extensions-closest draw, 3 t/part busy, **+1 e/t self-charge below 300** (the floor of every bootstrap oracle), extension counts/capacities by RCL re-derived per tick from the CURRENT controller level (room max 12,900 @RCL8), queue = bid-ordered head-of-line banking (the shared kernel), request order deterministic by `(spawn_idx, submission index)`.
3. **Economy (stores/transfer/build/repair/decay):** per-resource stores; adjacency-1 intents; transfer-class resolves before harvest (the within-creep `creepActions` order); a Transfer over-asking the have-side rejects whole; TTL death at the `ageTime−1` boundary drops stores; build 5 progress/WORK/t @1 e/progress against the plan's `required_rcl` schedule; repair 100 hits/WORK/t @1 e/WORK/t; road decay 100/1000t (×5 swamp) **+ `ROAD_WEAROUT` per-step traffic wear** (the engine's per-step timer pull, not damage×parts) — which regenerates the repair bait honestly *and* feeds the repair bid's wear term; container decay; dropped decay.
4. **Controller/RCL:** 1 e/WORK/t upgrade, progress table, downgrade clock (+100/action, level-up resets to *half*-max, level-down halves progress). **Labs/minerals (FULL scope per operator):** reactions (5-in-from-each/5-out per per-compound `REACTION_TIME`), lab stores of 3000 mineral + 2000 energy (`LAB_MINERAL_CAPACITY` / `LAB_ENERGY_CAPACITY` — the per-lab ceilings that bound reaction and boost batching), boost costs (30 mineral + 20 energy/part), **boost EFFECTS on the economy loop** in the engine's exact two-step form (`upgradeController.js:33-92` / `build.js:67-83`: energy charged = the UNBOOSTED effect, PROGRESS gains the boosted effect via `BOOSTS[WORK]` ×1.5/1.8/2.0 — *not* the ×1/2/3/4 action ladder), and **terminal mineral-sale-to-energy as a recovery lever** — with their own metrics: compound time-to-X and boost e/t-equivalent (which also feeds the 0033 military-w arm). Conservation stays EXACT per resource: mineral harvest mints, reactions transmute, boosts and sales burn, all ledgered.

**Metrics** (e/t currency; `η = T*/T ∈ (0,1]`, `H = ΣWη/ΣW` + seeded-bootstrap 95% CI via `stats.rs`; H regression-tracked, never a ==1 gate; statistical gates are N-seeded paired diffs vs stored (scenario, seed, SHA) baselines per EP-6.7; oracles are loose lower bounds — paired diffs are the instrument):

- **T_recover / H_recover (HEADLINE):** collapse state (RCL R structures per plan, creeps wiped or skeleton, spawns/extensions 0, storage S0 ∈ {0,5k,50k}, roads pre-decayed, clock parameterized) → recovered state. The official recovered state is **lane-only**: `energy_available == capacity` sustained for `RECOVER_FULL_WINDOW` (50) consecutive ticks (decision #7). Oracle: the sequential dependency chain (self-charge → first body → harvest ramp → 3 t/part fleet build-out → deficit refill). η=0 on non-recovery; `W = T*` with a 25%-per-family single-scenario weight cap.
- **T_RCL(N) / H_rcl (HEADLINE):** greenfield rush curves, N=2..4 fast / 5..6 full; conservation-bound oracle. The baseline runs at 13–38% of that bound — the market's quantified headroom.
- **repair_leak_e (THE SYMPTOM):** energy into repair while refill deficit exists, by class — live (S1 counter) and in-sim. **Repro gate:** baseline > 0 on bait scenarios AND T_recover inflated vs no-bait control (paired-seed, CI excluding zero) — no cure is scored until the sim shows the disease.
- **Market diagnostics:** `match_optimality_gap` (greedy vs exact oracle); `cpu_matching_ops/tick`; bid distributions per sink class; **flap_rate** (assignment/allowance churn per kilotick — the no-hysteresis directive's observation channel); **intent count per tick** (a policy must not win H by emitting more intents).
- **Refill diagnostics:** `spawn_energy_blocked_ticks` (+ refill-latency distribution), `spawn_idle_frac`, `extension_deficit_integral`.
- **Self-sufficiency diagnostic (reported, never gating):** trailing-300t harvested income ≥ `RECOVER_INCOME_FRAC` (0.9) × source potential. It measures *activity*, not recovery — see decision #7.
- **Hard gates:** exact per-tick **energy conservation audit** (sources {harvest, self-charge, stock} − sinks {bodies, upgrade, build, repair by class, decay, drops, death} − Δstocks == 0; any imbalance fails the run as a sim bug); deadlock sentinel (12 zero-progress ticks with demand → η=0, zero occurrences gated); death-spiral flag (diagnostic). **Determinism fence (SHIP-BLOCKER):** `econ_is_deterministic_over_rounds` (5 seeded runs, spread 0) + `det_reorder`, green from the first commit; economy state is map-shaped — BTreeMap/sorted-boundary discipline from day 0.

**Scenario families** over the 13 captured foreman layouts: **C — collapse** (the axes above; road health is the discriminator, regenerated by traffic wear); **G — greenfield rush**; **S — steady-state guard rail** (healthy rooms incl. **low-RCL**, TTL churn, 10k ticks: refill latency, spawn idle, **road-stock health** must not regress — a market that wins C by never repairing fails here; also where emergent low-riding road equilibrium is *observed and accepted or rejected on evidence*); **D — downgrade pressure** (clock at 10%: the refill-vs-controller triage; level-down halves progress); **M — contended matching** (the family that measures `match_optimality_gap` under real carrier contention).

### §D8 Decisions (numbered)

1. **v0 bid constants** (`V_UPGRADE`, build-class table, refill ROI shape, repair imminence margin): shapes as specified above, numbers set by the sim sweep per EP-4.6. The refill floor is **derived** from the banking model's instant-spawnability premium rather than swept — the literal `max(1000, ROI)` shape was disconfirmed by the tournament.
2. **`can_afford_military` 20% reserve:** kept as a transition guardrail through soak, then deleted (EP-2.6). Flat S1 constants stand alongside it: in healthy RCL≥4 rooms the S1 arm is digest-identical to baseline.
3. **`spawn_throughput` shared home:** moved to `screeps-econ-engine::spawn_queue`; the combat-decision copy is deleted and consumers repointed, so the econ sim never depends on a combat crate.
4. **Matching escalation trigger:** the `match_optimality_gap`/CPU frontier at which bounded-auction replaces greedy. Measured pooled gap on the contended family is 18‰ against a ~50‰ threshold ⇒ greedy stands.
5. **Bid observability surface:** per-room console `[market]` line (top unmet bids + opportunity floor) + seg-57, with a Glance-HUD row (ADR 0016) when that infrastructure exists. Required for the market to be operable.
6. **Traffic-wear estimation source (live):** observed trailing hits-deltas per structure (ephemeral cache), not an analytic route-traffic model — it measures truth, and there is no model to drift; the sim validates both.
7. **T_recover recovered state is LANE-ONLY** — spawn/extension energy == capacity sustained `RECOVER_FULL_WINDOW` (50) ticks; the `RECOVER_INCOME_FRAC=0.9` trailing-income condition is a reported diagnostic, never gating. Evidence: the 0.9 cliff measures *activity*, not recovery — a disease-triggered repairer-builder satisfies it EARLIER in the bait arm than in the control, inverting the paired repro diff at RCL 3 (E11N1-rcl3 mean ΔT −1372; corpus gate FAIL +338.9 CI [−415.2, +1106.6]) while lane-only keeps every scenario positive (fast +781.2 [+632.3, +939.6]; full +580.2 [+514.8, +645.9]); Family D reproduces the shape (D-rcl3 ΔT vs C −11,619). Full record at `screeps-econ-eval/src/metrics.rs` (`RecoverConsts` docs).

## Consequences

### Positive
- The reported symptom dissolves rather than gets patched: a post-wipe extension refill *prices* at ~10× a road top-up, so haulers, builders, towers, and the spawn queue all do the right thing from one number — and the same number keeps doing the right thing in regimes nobody hand-tuned (sieges, remote expansion, lab ramp-up).
- The economy gains what combat already has: one currency (0032), numeric bids on the request lane (0033's w-lane), EV-optimized assignment (P-AUCTION), and a pure decision crate the sim drives (combat-decision) — architectural convergence, not a bespoke economy exception.
- Bootstrap recovery and RCL rush become regression-tracked numbers with CIs; the repair leak becomes a live counter; S6 (capacity-sized bodies) is fixed by design in K4; every future economy policy question inherits the bench.
- Bands, tiers, `has_sufficient_energy`/`has_excess_energy`, the walls 10k gate, and the S1 stopgap are all deleted at adoption — strictly fewer mechanisms at end-state than today.

### Negative / costs
- Calibration is a real program: mispriced `V_UPGRADE` or refill ROI can chronically starve upgraders or over-hoard — the tournament + Family S/D guard rails + the downgrade veto are the containment, and default-on waits for them.
- The refactor is wide: seam extraction (every economy mission/job), ticket reshape (WFV bump — trivial by directive but still a loud reset), spawn conversion touching combat. Sequenced as independently-gated milestones with A/A parity between each.
- Emergent systems are harder to eyeball: the bid readout (#5) is mandatory, and roads riding visibly low is the accepted look of correct pricing (Family S is the evidence channel if it's actually wrong).
- **The market re-prices repair rather than suppressing it**, so `repair_leak_e` *rises* relative to the S1 gate while road stock gets healthier (`road_stock_min` 0.71→0.99). "Leak down on collapse" is deliberately not a success criterion; the counter stays as an observation channel, not a target.
- Before the seam lands, the sim's baseline arm is a transcription of current behavior (citation-pinned) — bounded mirror risk for the baseline arm only; the *candidate* arm is the real shared crate.

### CPU & tick-safety impact
Pricing is cheap (integer bid functions per materialized request, from fields `EconomyAssessmentSystem` already gathers). The matching pass is the one new hot-path cost: greedy `O(E log E)` over per-room candidate edges, with an explicit measured budget (≤ ~50 ops/tick/room) and the exact-assignment oracle confined to the sim. Under stress the bot emits *fewer* intents (repairs priced out, no repairer spawn, fewer tower shots) and harvesters stop forfeiting Pipeline-A harvest ticks. All touched systems keep their `StageClass`; pricing/matching feed haul/spawn (never-shed lane). Breaking-change labels (EP-5.2): the S1 gate and the seam-at-parity milestones are Behavioral (flag-gated) and need no format bump; the ticket reshape is Memory-format (loud reset) + Behavioral.

## End state

The civilian economy runs one ratified currency (energy-equivalent per tick) end-to-end: hauler selection prices every sink and assigns by raw bid-density (the market's greedy `market_pass`, not a tier sieve); repair admission is the opportunity floor; spawn requests bid in the same currency (civilian `body_roi`, military `p_win·value_e/est_ticks`); the `SPAWN_PRIORITY_*` f32 bands are replaced by the `SPAWN_BID_*` u32 milli-e/t ladder in `screeps-econ-decision`, and `TransferPriority`/`TransferType` live in `screeps-econ-decision::priority` — they still ride inside serialized tickets and drive the retained tier-interleave fallback, so they are consolidated, not removed. The offline `screeps-econ-engine`/`-decision`/`-eval` simulator (sim spawn / economy / source+mineral / controller / **full** lab systems) is the calibration + regression instrument, sharing the exact decision crate the bot runs.

Decision dispositions: #1 refill floor derived (banking-model instant-spawnability premium, not a swept constant); #2 flat S1 constants stand; #3 `spawn_throughput` home = `screeps-econ-engine`; #4 greedy matching (18‰ contended gap vs a ~50‰ threshold); #5 observability = console `[market]` line + seg-57, HUD row when ADR 0016's infra lands; #6 traffic wear = observed timer-pull; #7 recovered state = lane-only, income-frac demoted to a diagnostic.
