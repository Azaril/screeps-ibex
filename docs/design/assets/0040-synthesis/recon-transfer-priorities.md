# Energy transfer/haul priority system — recon findings

All paths relative to `C:\code\screeps-ibex\screeps-ibex\src`.

## 1. Full priority model

**Enums / orderings:**
- `TransferPriority { High=0, Medium=1, Low=2, None=3 }` (`transfer/transfersystem.rs:16-23`). `ACTIVE_TRANSFER_PRIORITIES = [High, Medium, Low]`; `ALL_TRANSFER_PRIORITIES` adds `None` (`:36-42`). Bitflag masks `TransferPriorityFlags {HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW, NONE, ACTIVE, ALL}` (`:44-57`). There is **no tier above High** and no numeric sub-priority — 4 coarse buckets.
- `TransferType { Haul, Link, Terminal, Use }` + flags (`:73-106`). `Haul` = creep hauling lane; `Link` = link router; `Use` = in-place consumption (upgraders/builders drawing from controller containers/links); `Terminal` = terminal send lane.
- `TransferTarget` covers Container/Spawn/Extension/Storage/Tower/Link/Ruin/Tombstone/Resource/Terminal/Lab/Factory/Nuker/PowerSpawn (`:108-124`).
- Requests are keyed `(resource, priority, allowed_type)`: `TransferWithdrawlKey` (`:456-460`) and `TransferDepositKey` (resource is `Option` — `None` = "accepts anything", used by storage) (`:470-475`). Each `TransferNode` (one per target structure) tracks `withdrawls/deposits` plus `pending_*` mirrors (`:490-495`); availability = requested − pending, floored at 0 (`:516-530`).
- The whole queue is **ephemeral**: `TransferQueueUpdateSystem` clears it every tick (`:2459-2466`); demand generators are re-registered per room per tick and flushed lazily by transfer-type (`LazyTransferQueueRooms`, `:1357-1438`). In-flight creep tickets re-register their pending amounts each tick in `pre_run_job`/`gather_data` (`jobs/haul.rs:146-152`, `:218-222`).

**Matching (hauler ↔ request):** decentralized, per-creep, greedy at decision time — there is no global assignment/auction.
- Priority-tier iteration: `generate_active_priorities` (`transfer/utility.rs:34-98`) yields alternating (pickup_mask, delivery_mask) pairs in the order **(ALL→HIGH-deliver), (HIGH-pickup→ALL), (ALL→MED), (MED→ALL), (ALL→LOW), (LOW→ALL), (ACTIVE→NONE), (NONE→ACTIVE)** — i.e. "deliver-to-High from any source" is tried first. Consumed by `select_pickup_and_delivery` (`transfersystem.rs:2154-2188`), which returns the **first tier pass that produces any pair**.
- Within a tier pass, `select_best_delivery` (`:1795-1877`) scores every (pickup, delivery) candidate as `value = total_resources / (linear_range(creep→pickup) + linear_range(pickup→delivery))` (`finite_transfer_value`, `:30-34`; `:1855-1875`) and takes the max — amount-per-distance greedy, **priority is only a bucket filter, never a tiebreak inside the mask**.
- For a creep already carrying resources, `select_deliveries` (`:1674-1710`) collects all nodes matching the whole allowed mask **flat** (no per-priority ordering) and the caller picks the **nearest** ticket (`find_nearest_linear_by`, `jobs/utility/haulbehavior.rs:164-175`).
- Double-booking is prevented only by the pending bookkeeping (`register_pickup`/`register_delivery` → `pending_withdrawls/pending_deposits`, `transfersystem.rs:587-617`); creeps that pick earlier in the tick claim the demand.

## 2. Spawn/extension refill demand

Generated by `RoomTransferMission`'s haul generator (`missions/localsupply/room_transfer.rs:239-267`, registered `:714-730`):
- Spawns: deposit `TransferPriority::High`, amount = free energy capacity (`request_transfer_for_spawns`, `:426-443`).
- Extensions: deposit `TransferPriority::High`, amount = free capacity (`request_transfer_for_extension`, `:445-462`).

**The priority is completely static.** Always `High`, regardless of `room.energy_available()`, RCL, wipe/recovery state, or spawn-queue pressure. There is no escalation path (and no tier above High to escalate into). Meanwhile **other demand shares the High bucket**: towers when hostiles present (`missions/tower.rs:176-191`), wall-repair siege tower refill (`missions/wall_repair.rs:126-140`), tombstone loot >200 (`room_transfer.rs:645-649`), dropped resources >500 (`:672-676`). Within the shared bucket, nearest/value wins — a spawn has no precedence over a tower.

## 3. Hauler task selection (exact logic)

`HaulState::Idle::tick` (`jobs/haul.rs:57-140`), in order:
1. **Deliver current cargo, ACTIVE mask** (`:86-95`) → `get_new_delivery_current_resources_state` (`haulbehavior.rs:129-179`): collects High+Medium+Low deposits **flattened**, picks the **nearest**. Registered, becomes `Delivery`.
2. **Deliver current cargo, NONE mask** (`:96-107`) — dump to storage/containers.
3. **New pickup+delivery, full capacity, ALL/ALL** (`:108-127`) → `get_new_pickup_and_delivery_full_capacity_state` → `select_pickup_and_delivery` tier interleave (see §1), then `get_additional_deliveries` chains secondary deposits at ALL priorities within range 10 of the last delivery (`haulbehavior.rs:248-364`).
4. Move to invisible pickup room (`:128-138`); else `Wait(5)`.
- In `Pickup` state, every 5th tick the hauler tops off its ticket with **additional deliveries at `TransferPriorityFlags::ALL`** for leftover capacity (`jobs/haul.rs:163-207`).
- `Delivery` state: optional opportunistic repair, then `tick_delivery` walks the ticket list front-to-back (`haulbehavior.rs:516-578`).

**Yes — a hauler carrying energy can service a lower-priority sink while spawns/extensions are empty**, via several routes:
- **(a) Nearest-wins inside the ACTIVE flat mask** (step 1): a hauler holding energy next to a Medium/Low sink (lab Medium `missions/labs.rs:431-439`; terminal Medium/Low `missions/terminal.rs:336-401`; tower Low when peaceful `tower.rs:179`; controller container Low `room_transfer.rs:352-366`; powerspawn fraction-mapped) delivers there rather than crossing the room to a High spawn — priority is not consulted inside the mask, only distance (`haulbehavior.rs:154-175`).
- **(b) Secondary-delivery chaining at ALL priorities** (steps 3 and Pickup top-off): remaining capacity is committed to `None`-priority sinks (storage `room_transfer.rs:487-495`, controller containers) within range 10 — reasonable, but it means energy physically on the creep is pledged to storage while new High demand appearing next tick must wait for the next idle hauler.
- **(c) Opportunistic repair burns delivery-earmarked energy**: multi-room haulers (`allow_repair = max_distance > 0`, `missions/haul.rs:295-296`) repair any ≥Low-priority structure (roads included) within range 3 mid-delivery and mid-transit, decrementing the deposit tickets (`jobs/haul.rs:229-233`, `:246-248`; `consume_resource_from_deposits` `transfersystem.rs:1124-1134`). Harvesters do the same at ≥Medium while **delivering to spawns** (`jobs/harvest.rs:264-266`) and while harvesting (`:225`); static miners at ≥Low (`jobs/staticmine.rs:144`). A road at <25% health maps to `RepairPriority::High`, <50% Medium (`jobs/utility/repair.rs:23-37`), so ordinary decayed roads qualify. This is the operator-observed "repairs roads instead of refilling" behavior — plus `BuildJob` idles into repair **before** even fetching energy (see §5).
- **(d) Remote haulers never refill spawns at all**: `storage_delivery_only = max_distance > 0` restricts them to Container/Storage/Terminal (`missions/haul.rs:296`; `target_filters::storage` `transfersystem.rs:334-339`).

## 4. Reservation / starvation-prevention / anti-diversion

**In the transfer lane: none beyond double-booking prevention.** The pending-ticket bookkeeping (`transfersystem.rs:587-617`) stops two creeps fulfilling the same request; nothing earmarks energy (in storage, containers, or on creeps) for spawning, and nothing stops builders/upgraders draining the exact supply a spawn refill needs (§5). The explicit TODO exists: `missions/haul.rs:299` — "TODO: Make sure there is handling for starvation/bootstrap mode."

What *does* exist, adjacent:
- **Spawn-queue head-of-line energy banking**: `process_room_spawns` sorts requests descending by `f32` priority and `break`s on the first request with `body_cost > available_energy` (but ≤ capacity), reserving room energy for it against lower-priority spawns (`spawnsystem.rs:423-436`; band rationale documented `:22-39`). This banks *already-in-structures* energy for the top spawn, but does nothing to make haulers deliver it.
- **Bootstrap body sizing**: with zero haulers, the first hauler is sized to `energy_available` not capacity (`missions/haul.rs:229-237`); same pattern for first builder (`missions/localbuild.rs:255-259`) and first upgrader under downgrade risk (`missions/upgrade.rs:292-296`, priority CRITICAL `:319-322`). Local miners spawn at CRITICAL→HIGH (`localsupply/source_mining.rs:401-406`).
- Renew gated behind spawn requests and a 10k stored-energy floor (`spawnsystem.rs:19-20`, `:407-412`, `:472-513`).

## 5. Build/repair energy in the same lane

**Yes — builders, repairers, and upgraders withdraw from exactly the supply nodes haulers use for spawn refill, with no arbitration:**
- `BuildJob::Idle` order is: repair(≥High) → build → repair(any) → **then** pickup energy at `TransferPriorityFlags::ALL`, `HAUL|USE` (`jobs/build.rs:56-103`); pickup ticket sized to full free capacity (`haulbehavior.rs:13-56`), nearest-supply wins. That mask matches every withdraw node: source containers (Medium/Low/None by fullness, `room_transfer.rs:316-336`), storage (None, `:464-482`), storage links (High/Low/None, `:518-541`), tombstones/dropped (High/Medium, `:637-688`). First-registered wins against a hauler wanting the same energy for a spawn.
- `UpgradeJob` identical mask, anchored near the controller (`jobs/upgrade.rs:112-122`, `:171-181`).
- `HarvestJob` (the bootstrap generalist) prefers High deliveries first (`jobs/harvest.rs:165-174`) but then **upgrade → build → repair(≥Medium) before Medium/Low/None deliveries** (`:175-185`), and its hauling arm can pair source-pickups straight into storage.
- Repairs themselves never request energy through the transfer queue (the `RepairQueue` is a target list, `localbuild.rs:198-213`); the energy leaves via creep pickups above and via opportunistic repair (§3c). `LocalBuildMission` spawns a repairer at `SPAWN_PRIORITY_HIGH` whenever any ≥High repair target exists (`localbuild.rs:112-122`) — a decayed road can trigger a HIGH-band spawn competing for the same spawn energy.

## 6. Existing stress/emergency modulation in this lane

- Tower deposit priority: High iff hostiles present, else Low (`missions/tower.rs:176-180`).
- Wall-repair siege generator: only under hostiles + wall below `EMERGENCY_WALL_HITS` → towers at High (`missions/wall_repair.rs:111-140`).
- Controller-link demand escalates as its buffer empties (High<25% … defers to None ≥75%) — the one place where deposit priority is a function of state (`room_transfer.rs:56-93`, `:577-616`).
- Source-container withdraw priority scales with fullness (`room_transfer.rs:316-323`); storage-link withdraw likewise (`:522-530`).
- `HaulMission` scales hauler count and spawn-priority from measured unfulfilled haul volume (20-tick cached `total_unfufilled_resources`, `missions/haul.rs:100-117`, `:271-291`).
- `UpgradeMission` downgrade-risk → `SPAWN_PRIORITY_CRITICAL` (`missions/upgrade.rs:319-322`).
- `LocalBuildMission` collapses to 1 builder when stored/container energy is low (`localbuild.rs:227-244`).
- `EconomySnapshot` (per-room `stored_energy`, `energy_income`) with a military spending reserve `can_afford_military` (`military/economy.rs:27-31`, `:82-84`) — a ready-made stress signal, but **nothing in the transfer lane reads it**.

**Notably absent:** no low-energy escalation of spawn/extension deposit priority; no priority tier above High; no energy reservation for spawning; the deliver-current-cargo path ignores priority order entirely inside the ACTIVE mask; opportunistic repair has no room-energy gate (only per-structure health thresholds and the storage>10k gate for VeryLow walls, `jobs/utility/repair.rs:84`).
